# Zomato Restaurant Market Analysis

## Project Overview

The Zomato Restaurant Market Analysis project explores restaurant-level data to understand customer preferences, restaurant performance, pricing segments, service adoption, cuisine trends, and market opportunities. The analysis combines Python, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw restaurant data into practical business insights.

## Business Objective

The project focuses on key marketplace questions:

- Which restaurant segments perform best?
- How do pricing and service offerings relate to restaurant performance?
- Which cities show stronger or weaker restaurant performance?
- Which cuisines are most common in the market?
- Does online delivery relate to customer engagement?
- Does table booking relate to restaurant ratings?
- Which market segments present potential business opportunities?

## Dataset

The analysis uses restaurant-level Zomato data containing information such as:

- Restaurant name and location
- Cuisine
- Average cost
- Aggregate rating
- Rating votes
- Online delivery
- Table booking
- Price range
- Restaurant and city information

The final analysis contains **9,551 restaurants**.
